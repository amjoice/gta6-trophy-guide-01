# Leonida Platinum — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Tracker

An offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI, built for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01) "Build the best GTA 6 trophy guide".

- **Tracker** — every trophy with its official name, description and grade; a checklist saved in the browser (localStorage) with export and import; filters by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, crime, side, world) and grade; a spoiler setting that hides story trophies until earned; counters toward the PSN targets for the count trophies.
- **Roadmap** — five stages to the platinum, in order, built from the official descriptions.
- **Missables** — the three partner trophies that can pass you by, flagged, plus the ones that are only easy to overlook.
- **Guides** — a guide to every trophy in our own words, each citing its sources (the official list on Exophase and the PSN counts on PSNProfiles). GTA 6 released 19 Nov 2026, so where the official description does not pin down a place or a step, the guide says so instead of inventing one.

The app is plain static files under `app/` — no build step, no network request, no Rockstar logo or official artwork. Serve it with `node serve.ts` and open http://localhost:8080.
